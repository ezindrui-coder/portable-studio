Dramatic

Mood: Intense • Powerful • Cinematic
Mission
Create a strong cinematic lighting setup with a dramatic visual impact.
Challenge
Experiment with contrast and light direction to emphasise the form and character of the subject.
Output
Capture ONE final photo with a strong dramatic effect.
A strong dramatic setup
  • One dominant hard light source from the side (side or Rembrandt lighting).
  • High contrast between the lit side and the shadow side.
  • Little to no fill light, so shadows stay deep.
  • Angle the light to sculpt the subject's shape and texture.
