Commercial

Mood: Clean • Attractive • Professional
Mission
Create clean lighting suitable for a product advertisement or promotional image.
Challenge
Control shadows, highlights and reflections while keeping the subject clearly visible.
Output
Capture ONE final photo suitable for use in a commercial advertisement.
A strong commercial setup
  • Even, soft light from the front or a 45° angle, with minimal harsh shadows.
  • A fill light or reflector to lift shadows on the opposite side.
  • Clean, neutral background so the subject stands out.
  • Watch for unwanted glare or reflections on shiny surfaces.
