Mystery

Mood: Secretive • Curious • Uncertain
Mission
Create a mysterious scene where lighting reveals only selected parts of the subject.
Challenge
Experiment with light and shadow to hide and reveal visual information.
Output
Capture ONE final photo that makes the viewer curious about the subject.
A strong mystery setup
  • One narrow, controlled light source, such as a snoot or a slit of light.
  • Most of the subject left in shadow, with only a small part lit.
  • Side or top-down light rather than a full, even light.
  • Keep colour restrained; let the shape of the light do the work.
