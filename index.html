How to play

Everything you need is in the briefcase. Play alone or in teams.

  1. Open the briefcaseTake out the lights, props and the card deck.
  2. Draw a cardRead the mood, mission and challenge out loud.
  3. Set up your scenePosition your subject, then experiment with Key, Fill and Back light.
  4. Adjust and observeChange position, brightness and colour, and watch how light, shadow and mood shift. There's no fixed lighting setup — experiment, observe and create.
  5. Capture ONE photoYou have 10 minutes. Only your final shot counts.
  6. Explain your choicesBe ready to explain your lighting decisions.
  7. Check the answerOpen that card's page below and compare your setup.

Tutorial

Four things you can change to shift the mood of any photo.

DirectionFront, side, top or below. Light from below feels unnatural.
ShadowsHard shadows add tension. Soft shadows calm a scene.
ContrastBig gaps between light and dark feel dramatic.
ColourWarm light feels safe. Cool, green or purple light feels off.
