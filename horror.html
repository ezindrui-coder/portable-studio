Horror

Mood: Fear • Suspense • Unsettling
Mission
Create a lighting setup that transforms the subject into a frightening or suspenseful scene.
Challenge
Experiment with shadows, lighting direction, contrast and colour to create tension.
Output
Capture ONE final photo that clearly communicates a horror mood.
A strong horror setup
  • Light from below or from one hard side, never from the front.
  • Hard, dark shadows across the face or subject.
  • High contrast, with most of the frame left in darkness.
  • A cool or tinted colour such as green, blue or purple.
