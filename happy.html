Happy

Mood: Bright • Cheerful • Positive
Mission
Create a lighting setup that makes the subject look bright, cheerful and positive.
Challenge
Experiment with light position, brightness and colour to minimise harsh shadows.
Output
Capture ONE final photo that clearly communicates a happy mood.
A strong happy setup
  • Bright, even, front-facing light with soft shadows.
  • A fill light or reflector so no side is left dark.
  • Warm colour tones, such as soft yellow or natural white.
  • Higher overall brightness with low contrast.
